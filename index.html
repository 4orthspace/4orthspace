The idea — 01 / The fourth space

Sociologists named three places that hold a life. We built the fourth — where the work you're proud of actually gets made.

First place

Home

Where you rest. Comfortable, but rarely where momentum is found.

Second place

Work

Where you're paid. Structured, scheduled, and shaped by someone else.

Third place

The social

Cafés, clubs, lounges. Welcome, but designed for everyone at once.

Fourth place — 4orth

Here

Curated, private, and tuned for output. Where ambition is finally at home.

Picture & video policy

To protect every member's privacy and focus, filming at 4orth is limited to close-ups. You may photograph or film your own desk and laptop, your drinks, and details of the space and its design. Please don't shoot vlogs, pan across the general work area, or capture other members, their screens or their conversations. If you're unsure, ask the team first.

Good to know — 07

Questions, answered.

Day passes and meeting rooms are open to all by reservation. Resident, Signature, and Executive memberships are by application — a short conversation that keeps the community considered and the rooms quiet.

Every tier includes secure access, gigabit fibre with backup power, printing, a monthly allocation of meeting-room hours, and access to member events. Signature and Executive add a business address, call handling, and dedicated space. We're still in pre-opening, so set access hours will be confirmed before we open.

Yes. Signature and Executive members receive monthly guest passes, and any member can book private meeting rooms for client sessions. The ground-floor lounge is designed precisely for those first impressions.

We're in Guzape, Abuja, with secure on-site parking for members and validated guest parking. The full address is shared on confirmation of your tour.

Monthly plans roll month to month and can be paused or cancelled with 14 days' notice. Annual plans can be transferred or upgraded at any time — speak to your concierge and we'll handle the rest.
